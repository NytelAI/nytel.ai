Nytel protocol compiler: results

November 2026

Nytel's compiler reads a clinical trial protocol PDF and builds the study: visits, windows, cycles, forms, fields and visit conditions. It writes a CDISC USDM 4.0 study model, CDISC ODM 1.3.2, REDCap import files and a build workbook. Every item it adds cites its protocol page.

Reading protocols

In a blind test on 80 pages from 10 oncology protocols, the reader reproduced 99.5% of table cells, 96.7% of headings and 98.6% of words. The automated schedule-of-activities pass reproduced 3,036 schedule cells from 10 protocols with 99.8% agreement and no footnote-mark errors.

Protocol to study build

Nytel has built EDC specifications for 58 protocols. All 58 produce valid CDISC ODM 1.3.2 and REDCap import files that load without errors. Nytel extends the USDM standard so that visit conditions are converted into executable rules.

Against real EDC builds

On 9 oncology trials with a posted EDC or CRF build, Nytel's builds hold 87% of the fields the protocol asks for and 94% of the forms, and 86% of Nytel's fields match the real build or are supported by the protocol.

Coverage of what the protocol requires

On 8 trials with a posted EDC or CRF build, Nytel's builds collect 89% to 100% of the protocol's required elements, including endpoints and every assessment at every visit. That is as good as or better than the hand-built EDC on 6 of 8.

Ready to load and edit

Generated REDCap import files have 0 variable-name errors on 9 of 9 builds. Reviewer edits are preserved across rebuilds on 9 of 9 builds.

Speed

About 33 agent minutes per protocol.

Method

Test sets are publicly posted protocols from ClinicalTrials.gov, with their posted EDC or CRF builds where compared. Independent model judges labelled and matched fields, with a second-judge audit.
