Fair comparisons

Why do trials flip a coin to decide who gets the drug?

Randomization is the coin flip that assigns treatment. You can try a version of it in module 0, the randomization test.

Two futures per patient

Every patient has two possible futures: one if they take the drug, one if they do not. A real trial sees only one of them, as in the faded cells of the module 0 game.

Here you see both, so you can check the trial against the truth. Each column is one patient: top person is the outcome with the drug, bottom person is the outcome without it. The small mark under them shows hidden health.

Why the gap is not the drug

The gap you see is the drug's effect on the people who took it, plus how different the groups already were.

In symbols: observed gap E[Y|D=1] − E[Y|D=0] = effect on the treated + selection bias, E[Y₀|D=1] − E[Y₀|D=0]. See Angrist and Pischke (2009).

Quit the drug? Still counted. ITT

Assumption for this picture: the drug adds 10 points for anyone who takes it, so quitters dilute the group average.

These are two different questions (an estimand). "What if we offer the drug?" counts everyone. "What if everyone takes it?" needs other methods, not dropping people.

Open label, yet still fair: death cannot be colored by hope.

PANORAMIC: molnupiravir

26,411 patients were randomized. About 1 in 100 in each group was hospitalized or died. Same rate in both groups, so no gain.

Butler et al., Lancet 2023;401:281-293 (via Sokolova and Sokolov, 2026). A clear "no" is a useful answer.

Hormone therapy (WHI): Rossouw et al., JAMA 2002;288:321-333, shown in the last card above (+29% heart attacks, +41% strokes).

Regulator's corner

"Preservation of the initial randomisation in analysis is important in preventing bias"

ICH E9, the statistics guideline of the ICH, section 5.2.1. Read the guideline (PDF). Why rules like this exist (thalidomide) is in module 10.

Takeaway

  • If people choose, the groups differ before the drug does anything. A coin flip makes them alike on average.
  • Hide who got what, so hopes cannot color the results.
  • Count everyone as randomized, even people who quit.
